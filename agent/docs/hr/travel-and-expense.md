# Travel and Expense Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out how business travel is approved and booked, and how business expenses are reimbursed at Lumora Systems.

## General Principles

- Employees should spend company money as if it were their own: reasonably, honestly and only for legitimate business purposes.
- All expenses must be supported by itemised receipts and submitted through the expense module in LumoraHR.
- Expense claims must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after **60 days** will not be reimbursed unless approved by the CFO.

## Travel Approval

- **Domestic travel** must be approved in advance by the employee's manager.
- **International travel** must be approved in advance by the manager and the department head, at least **14 days** before the travel date where possible.
- All travel must be booked through the company's travel desk (travel@lumora-systems.example), which arranges flights, accommodation and visas.

## Air Travel

- Economy class is used for all flights.
- **Premium economy** may be booked for flights longer than **6 hours** for employees at job level L6 and above.
- **Business class** requires CEO approval.

## Accommodation

- Accommodation is booked by the travel desk in company-approved hotels.
- Nightly accommodation limits are:
  - Sri Lanka: LKR 35,000
  - Singapore: SGD 300
  - United Kingdom: GBP 200
  - Other countries: USD 200
- Employees may stay with friends or family instead, but no accommodation allowance is paid in that case.

## Per Diem

- A daily per diem is paid for international travel to cover meals and incidental expenses:
  - Singapore: **SGD 80**
  - United Kingdom: **GBP 60**
  - Other countries: **USD 60**
- For domestic travel that requires an overnight stay, the per diem is **LKR 6,000** per day.
- Per diem is reduced by 30% for each day on which meals are provided by the hotel or a client.

## Ground Transport

- Taxis or ride-hailing services may be used for business travel. Public transport is encouraged where practical.
- Employees using their personal vehicle for business travel are reimbursed at **LKR 110 per kilometre**, with mileage logged in the expense claim.

## Client Entertainment

- Client entertainment must have a clear business purpose and requires prior manager approval.
- The limit is **LKR 12,000 per person** (or equivalent abroad), including the employee.
- The names of the guests and their organisations must be recorded in the expense claim.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed:

- Alcohol, except as part of approved client entertainment.
- Traffic fines, parking fines and penalties.
- Personal entertainment, such as in-room movies or minibar items.
- Upgrades not approved under this policy.
- Expenses for travelling companions.

## Corporate Credit Cards

- Corporate credit cards may be issued to employees who travel frequently, with approval from Finance.
- Corporate cards must not be used for personal expenses. Statements must be reconciled in LumoraHR within **10 days** of the statement date.

## Reimbursement

- Approved expense claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary if they are approved before the **15th of the month**.
