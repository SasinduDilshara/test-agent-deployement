# Employee Benefits Policy

## Overview

Lumora Systems provides benefits that support the health, wellbeing, learning and financial security of employees and their families. Benefits start on the employee's **first day of employment** unless stated otherwise.

## Medical Insurance

- All permanent employees are covered by a group medical insurance policy.
- **Hospitalisation cover**: up to **LKR 750,000** per person per policy year.
- **Outpatient (OPD) cover**: up to **LKR 60,000** per person per policy year, covering doctor consultations, prescribed medicines and diagnostic tests.
- **Spectacles**: up to **LKR 25,000** once every two policy years, included within the OPD limit.
- Coverage includes the employee's **spouse and up to 3 children** under the age of 21 at no cost to the employee.
- Parents can be added to the scheme at a subsidised premium deducted from salary.
- The policy year runs from **April 1 to March 31**.
- Claims are submitted through the insurer's mobile app within **60 days** of the date of treatment.

## Life and Personal Accident Insurance

- Group life insurance cover of **36 times** the monthly basic salary.
- Personal accident cover of **36 times** the monthly basic salary for accidental death or permanent disability.

## Wellness Programme

- A **Wellness Allowance of LKR 30,000** per financial year can be claimed for gym memberships, sports club fees, fitness classes, or mental-wellbeing apps.
- Employees have free, confidential access to an **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP)** offering up to **6 counselling sessions** per issue per year for employees and their immediate family members.
- An annual health screening is offered to all employees at partner hospitals.

## Learning and Development

- Each employee has a **Learning Allowance of LKR 150,000** per financial year for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets relevant to their role or career development.
- Expenses above LKR 50,000 for a single item require pre-approval from the manager.
- For company-sponsored degree or postgraduate programmes above LKR 150,000, a separate sponsorship application is required. Sponsored employees must sign a service agreement to remain with the company for **12 to 24 months** after completion, depending on the sponsored amount.
- All employees have access to the company's online learning platform at no cost.

## Meals and Transport

- Lunch is provided free of charge at the Colombo and Kandy offices on working days.
- Employees who work in the office after **20:00** can claim transport home by taxi through the company taxi account.

## Childcare Support

- Parents with children under 5 years of age can claim a **Childcare Allowance of LKR 10,000 per month** per child, for up to two children, on submission of a registered day-care invoice.

## Employee Referral Bonus

- Employees who refer a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus once the new employee **completes probation**:
  - Job levels L1 to L4: **LKR 75,000**
  - Job levels L5 and above: **LKR 150,000**
- Referral bonuses are not paid for referrals of former employees who left less than 12 months ago, or for hiring managers referring candidates into their own team.

## Long Service Awards

- Employees receive a long service award at **5, 10 and 15 years** of service, consisting of a gift voucher and recognition at the annual company event.

## Gratuity

- Employees who complete **5 or more years** of continuous service are entitled to gratuity on separation, calculated as **half a month's last drawn basic salary for each completed year of service**, in line with the Payment of Gratuity Act of Sri Lanka.

## Changes to Benefits

- Benefits are reviewed annually. Changes are communicated at least **30 days** before they take effect.
