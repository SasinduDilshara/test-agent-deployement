# Grievance and Disciplinary Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how employees can raise workplace grievances and how disciplinary matters are handled fairly and consistently at Lumora Systems.

## Principles

- Matters are dealt with promptly, confidentially and without unreasonable delay.
- Employees have the right to know the allegations against them and to respond before any decision is made.
- Employees may be accompanied by a colleague of their choice at formal grievance and disciplinary meetings.
- No disciplinary action is taken until the matter has been investigated.

## Raising a Grievance

1. **Informal stage**: employees are encouraged to raise concerns first with their manager, who should try to resolve them within **5 working days**.
2. **Formal stage**: if the matter is not resolved, or if it concerns the manager, the employee may submit a written grievance to People & Culture through LumoraHR (*Employee Relations > Raise a Grievance*) or by email to people@lumora-systems.example.
3. People & Culture acknowledges a formal grievance within **2 working days** and holds a grievance meeting within **10 working days**.
4. A written outcome is provided within **5 working days** of the meeting.
5. The employee may appeal the outcome in writing within **7 working days** to the Chief People Officer, whose decision is final.

Complaints of harassment are handled by the Anti-Harassment Committee as described in the Code of Conduct.

## Disciplinary Process

1. **Investigation**: an independent investigator is appointed to gather facts. The employee may be suspended on full pay during an investigation where this is necessary to protect people or evidence. Suspension is not a disciplinary action.
2. **Notice of hearing**: the employee receives a written notice describing the allegations and the evidence at least **3 working days** before the hearing.
3. **Disciplinary hearing**: the employee is given the opportunity to respond to the allegations.
4. **Decision**: the decision and any sanction are confirmed in writing within **5 working days** of the hearing.
5. **Appeal**: the employee may appeal within **7 working days** of the decision. Appeals are heard by a manager more senior than the original decision maker.

## Disciplinary Sanctions

Depending on the seriousness of the matter, sanctions may include:

- **Verbal warning**: recorded on file for **6 months**.
- **First written warning**: recorded on file for **12 months**.
- **Final written warning**: recorded on file for **18 months**.
- **Dismissal**: termination of employment, with or without notice depending on the circumstances.

## Gross Misconduct

Gross misconduct may lead to dismissal without prior warnings. Examples include:

- Theft, fraud or deliberate falsification of records, including expense claims.
- Violence, threats of violence, or serious bullying or harassment.
- Serious breaches of information security or deliberate disclosure of confidential information.
- Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs.
- Accepting or offering bribes.
- Serious breaches of health and safety rules.

## Records

- Records of grievances and disciplinary actions are kept confidentially by People & Culture in line with data protection requirements.
