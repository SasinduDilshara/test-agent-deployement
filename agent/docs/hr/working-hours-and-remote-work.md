# Working Hours and Remote Work Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets out standard working hours, flexible working arrangements, and the rules for working remotely at Lumora Systems.

## Standard Working Hours

- The standard working week is **40 hours**, Monday to Friday.
- A standard working day is **8 hours** excluding a **1-hour** meal break.
- **Core hours** are **10:00 to 15:00** local office time. All employees are expected to be available for meetings and collaboration during core hours unless they are on approved leave.
- Outside core hours, employees may choose their start and end times, provided they work their contracted hours and meet their team's commitments.

## Hybrid Work Model

- Lumora Systems operates a **hybrid** work model.
- Employees are expected to work from their assigned office for at least **2 days per week**. Each team agrees its **anchor days** (the days on which the whole team is in the office) and records them in the team charter.
- New employees are expected to work from the office for at least **3 days per week during their first 3 months** to support onboarding.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

- A fully remote arrangement may be approved for roles that do not require a physical presence in the office.
- Requests are submitted in LumoraHR and require approval from the employee's manager, the department head, and the People & Culture Business Partner.
- Fully remote arrangements are reviewed every **6 months**.
- Employees on fully remote arrangements must still attend in person for quarterly planning events and any mandatory in-person training, with travel costs reimbursed under the Travel and Expense Policy.

## Working from Another Country

- Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to **30 calendar days** per calendar year.
- Approval must be obtained at least **14 days in advance** from the manager and People & Culture, so that tax, immigration and data-protection implications can be checked.
- Working from another country without approval is not permitted.

## Remote Work Equipment and Allowance

- Every employee receives a company laptop and standard accessories (headset, monitor on request) from IT & Security.
- Employees on hybrid or fully remote arrangements receive a monthly **Internet and Home Office Allowance of LKR 8,000**, paid with the monthly salary.
- A one-time **home office setup allowance of LKR 40,000** is available to fully remote employees for a desk and an ergonomic chair. Receipts must be submitted through the expense system.

## Information Security While Working Remotely

- Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems from outside the office.
- Confidential information must not be discussed or displayed in public places.
- Company devices must be locked when unattended and must not be used by family members or other third parties.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk (helpdesk@lumora-systems.example) within **2 hours** of discovery.

## Overtime and On-Call

- Employees should not routinely work more than their contracted hours. Overtime must be pre-approved by the manager.
- Overtime pay and on-call allowances are described in the Compensation and Payroll Policy.
- Employees who work late into the night (after 22:00) on production incidents may start late the following day without needing to take leave.

## Attendance Recording

- Attendance is recorded automatically through office access cards on office days.
- Remote working days are recorded by marking *Remote* in LumoraHR under *Time > Work Location* before 10:00 on the day.
