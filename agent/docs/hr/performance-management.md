# Performance Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how performance is planned, reviewed, rated and rewarded at Lumora Systems.

## Performance Cycle

The performance cycle follows the financial year (**April 1 to March 31**) and has three stages:

1. **Goal setting (April)**: employees and managers agree 3 to 6 goals, aligned with team and company objectives (OKRs), and record them in LumoraHR by **April 30**.
2. **Mid-year review (October)**: a structured check-in on progress, development and wellbeing, completed by **October 31**. No rating is given at mid-year.
3. **Annual review (March to April)**: self-assessment, peer feedback, manager assessment, calibration and final rating.

## Continuous Feedback

- Managers hold **one-to-one meetings** with each direct report at least **every two weeks**.
- Employees can give and request feedback at any time through LumoraHR. Peer feedback is visible to the recipient and their manager.

## Annual Review Process

1. **Self-assessment**: the employee reflects on goals, achievements and the company values.
2. **Peer feedback**: the employee nominates 3 to 5 colleagues, and the manager may add others.
3. **Manager assessment**: the manager proposes a rating with written justification.
4. **Calibration**: department heads and People & Culture review ratings across teams to make sure they are fair and consistent.
5. **Review conversation**: the manager shares the final rating, feedback and development plan with the employee by **May 15**.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations and is a role model for others. |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds expectations in most areas. |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Fully meets the expectations of the role. |
| 2 | Partially Meets Expectations | Meets some expectations; improvement is needed in specific areas. |
| 1 | Does Not Meet Expectations | Performance is consistently below what is required for the role. |

Employees who joined after **December 31** of the cycle year are not rated in that cycle, but they receive feedback.

## Link to Rewards

- Ratings determine eligibility and multipliers for the annual performance bonus and salary increments, as described in the Compensation and Payroll Policy.
- Employees rated **4 or 5** in two consecutive cycles are considered for promotion where there is a business need.

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

- An employee rated **1**, or rated **2** for two consecutive cycles, is placed on a **Performance Improvement Plan** lasting **60 to 90 days**.
- The PIP sets specific, measurable objectives and the support that will be provided. Progress is reviewed at least every two weeks.
- If the objectives are not met at the end of the PIP, the matter may be handled under the Grievance and Disciplinary Policy.

## Appeals

- An employee who disagrees with their final rating may submit an appeal to their skip-level manager and People & Culture within **10 working days** of the review conversation.
- The appeal is reviewed by a panel that does not include the original manager, and the decision is communicated within **15 working days**.
