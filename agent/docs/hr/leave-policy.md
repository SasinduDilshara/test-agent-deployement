# Leave Policy

## Purpose and Scope

This policy explains the types of leave available to employees of Lumora Systems, how leave is accrued, and how to apply for it. It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees employed in Sri Lanka. Employees in the Singapore and London offices follow local addenda that are issued by People & Culture, which apply in addition to this policy where local law requires more generous terms.

Interns and consultants are not covered by this policy and should refer to their engagement letters.

## Leave Year

The leave year runs from **January 1 to December 31**. Leave entitlements are credited on January 1 each year. Employees who join during the year receive a pro-rated entitlement for annual leave in their first calendar year, as described below.

## Annual Leave

- Permanent employees are entitled to **18 working days** of paid annual leave per leave year.
- Employees with **5 or more years** of continuous service receive **21 working days** per leave year.
- In the first calendar year of employment, annual leave is pro-rated by the joining quarter:
  - Joined January 1 to March 31: 14 days
  - Joined April 1 to June 30: 10 days
  - Joined July 1 to September 30: 7 days
  - Joined October 1 to December 31: 4 days
- Annual leave must be requested at least **7 calendar days in advance** for absences of up to 3 days, and at least **21 calendar days in advance** for absences of 4 or more consecutive working days.
- Managers may decline or reschedule annual leave requests during declared release freeze periods; such periods are announced at least 30 days in advance.

## Carry Forward and Encashment

- Up to **5 days** of unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next leave year.
- Carried-forward leave must be used by **March 31** of the following year, after which it lapses.
- Annual leave is not encashable during employment. On separation, unused annual leave for the current leave year is paid out in the final settlement as described in the Resignation and Offboarding Policy.

## Casual Leave

- Employees are entitled to **7 working days** of paid casual leave per leave year for personal matters that cannot be planned in advance.
- Casual leave can be taken in half-day units.
- Casual leave cannot be carried forward and is not encashable.
- Casual leave should be informed to the manager before the start of the working day on which it is taken.

## Sick Leave

- Employees are entitled to **14 working days** of paid sick leave per leave year.
- A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of **more than 2 consecutive working days**.
- Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward and is not encashable.
- Where sick leave is exhausted, an employee with a long-term illness may apply for **extended medical leave** of up to 30 additional working days at 50% pay, subject to approval by the Chief People Officer and a medical review.

## Maternity Leave

- Female employees are entitled to **84 working days** of fully paid maternity leave for the first and second live births.
- For the third and subsequent live births, the entitlement is **42 working days** of fully paid maternity leave.
- Maternity leave may begin up to 4 weeks before the expected date of delivery.
- On returning to work, a mother is entitled to **one hour of nursing time per working day** until the child reaches **12 months** of age.
- Employees may combine maternity leave with annual leave to extend their time off, subject to manager approval.

## Paternity Leave

- Male employees are entitled to **5 working days** of fully paid paternity leave, to be taken within **3 months** of the birth or adoption of a child.

## Adoption Leave

- Employees who legally adopt a child under 5 years of age are entitled to **42 working days** of fully paid adoption leave for the primary caregiver, and 5 working days for the secondary caregiver.

## Bereavement Leave

- Employees are entitled to **3 working days** of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, parent-in-law or grandparent).

## Marriage Leave

- Employees are entitled to **3 working days** of paid leave for their own wedding. This leave must be taken within one month of the date of the wedding.

## Study and Examination Leave

- Employees following a course of study that is approved under the Learning and Development allowance (see the Benefits Policy) may take up to **5 working days** of paid examination leave per leave year.

## Unpaid Leave

- Employees who have exhausted their annual and casual leave may request unpaid leave of up to **30 calendar days** per leave year.
- Unpaid leave requires approval from the manager and the People & Culture Business Partner.

## Public Holidays

- Employees in Sri Lanka observe the public, bank and mercantile holidays gazetted by the Government of Sri Lanka.
- Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a **compensatory day off** to be taken within 30 days, in addition to any overtime payment that applies under the Compensation and Payroll Policy.

## How to Apply for Leave

1. Submit the leave request in **LumoraHR** (the HR self-service portal) under *Time Off > Request Leave*.
2. Attach supporting documents where required (for example, a medical certificate).
3. The request is routed to the employee's line manager for approval.
4. Approved leave is reflected automatically in the employee's leave balance and in payroll.

Questions about leave balances can be raised with People & Culture at people@lumora-systems.example.
