# Onboarding and Probation Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how new employees are welcomed to Lumora Systems and how the probation period is managed.

## Before the First Day

- New employees receive a welcome email from People & Culture **at least 5 working days** before their start date, with details of their first day, their manager and their onboarding buddy.
- New employees must complete pre-joining documentation in LumoraHR before day one, including bank details, emergency contacts, EPF details (Sri Lanka), and copies of identity documents and educational certificates.
- IT & Security prepares the laptop and accounts so that they are ready on the first day.

## First Day

- The first day starts at **09:30** at the assigned office with a welcome session from People & Culture.
- New employees collect their laptop, access card and welcome pack, and meet their manager and onboarding buddy.

## Onboarding Programme

- **Week 1**: company orientation, mandatory compliance training (Code of Conduct, Information Security, Data Protection, Anti-Harassment), and setting up tools.
- **Weeks 2 to 4**: role-specific training planned by the manager; product training for all employees in customer-facing and engineering roles.
- **30-60-90 day plan**: the manager and new employee agree goals for the first 30, 60 and 90 days within the first week.
- All mandatory compliance training must be completed within **14 days** of joining.

## Onboarding Buddy

- Each new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team or a related team for the first **3 months**.
- The buddy helps with day-to-day questions, introductions, and understanding team practices. The buddy is not responsible for performance evaluation.

## Probation Period

- All new permanent employees serve a probation period of **6 months**.
- Employees promoted internally or transferring between departments do not serve a new probation period.
- During probation, either party may terminate employment by giving **2 weeks'** written notice.

## Probation Reviews

- The manager holds a **mid-probation review** at the end of month 3 and documents it in LumoraHR.
- A **final probation review** is held at least **2 weeks before** the end of the probation period.
- The outcome of the final review is one of:
  - **Confirmation** of employment;
  - **Extension** of probation by up to **3 months**, with a documented improvement plan (probation may be extended only once); or
  - **Non-confirmation**, in which case employment ends at the end of the probation period.
- Employees receive a written confirmation letter from People & Culture within **5 working days** of a confirmation decision.

## Benefits During Probation

- Medical insurance, life insurance and the wellness programme apply from the first day.
- Employees on probation are entitled to annual, casual and sick leave as described in the Leave Policy.
- The Learning Allowance is available from the first day, but sponsorship for degree programmes is only available after confirmation.
