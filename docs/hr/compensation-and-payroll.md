# Compensation and Payroll Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how employees at Lumora Systems are paid, the components of pay, statutory contributions, overtime, bonuses and annual salary reviews.

## Pay Cycle and Pay Date

- Salaries are paid **monthly**.
- The pay date is the **25th of each month**. If the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salaries are paid on the **preceding working day**.
- Salaries are paid by direct bank transfer to the account registered in LumoraHR.
- Changes to bank details must be submitted in LumoraHR before the **15th of the month** to take effect for that month's payroll.

## Payslips

- Payslips are published in LumoraHR under *Pay > Payslips* on the pay date.
- Payroll queries should be raised with payroll@lumora-systems.example within **30 days** of the pay date.

## Components of Pay

- **Basic salary**: the fixed monthly salary stated in the employment contract.
- **Fixed allowances**: for example, the Internet and Home Office Allowance described in the Working Hours and Remote Work Policy.
- **Variable pay**: overtime, on-call allowances, and bonuses as described in this policy.

## Salary Bands

- Every role is mapped to a job level (L1 to L9) and each level has a salary band with a minimum, midpoint and maximum.
- Salary bands are reviewed annually against market data.
- Employees can view their job level in LumoraHR. Salary band ranges are shared by the employee's manager on request.

## Statutory Contributions

For employees in Sri Lanka:

- **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)**: the employee contributes **8%** and the company contributes **12%** of the employee's gross earnings that are liable for EPF.
- **Employees' Trust Fund (ETF)**: the company contributes **3%** of the employee's gross earnings that are liable for ETF.
- **Income tax (APIT)**: income tax is deducted at source each month under the Advance Personal Income Tax scheme, according to the tax tables published by the Inland Revenue Department.

## Overtime

- Overtime applies to employees at job levels **L1 to L3** only.
- Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager in LumoraHR.
- Overtime on working days is paid at **1.5 times** the hourly rate.
- Overtime on weekends and public holidays is paid at **2 times** the hourly rate.
- The hourly rate is calculated as the monthly basic salary divided by 240.

## On-Call Allowance

- Employees on an approved on-call rota receive an on-call allowance of **LKR 5,000 per week** of on-call duty.
- In addition, time spent actively responding to an incident outside working hours is paid as overtime for employees at L1 to L3, or compensated with time off in lieu for employees at L4 and above.

## Annual Performance Bonus

- Lumora Systems operates an annual performance bonus scheme linked to company performance and individual performance ratings.
- The target bonus ranges from **10% to 20%** of annual basic salary depending on job level.
- Bonuses are paid with the **June** salary, following the close of the financial year on March 31.
- Employees must be in active employment on the payment date and must have completed at least **3 months** of service in the financial year to be eligible. Bonuses for employees with less than a full year of service are pro-rated.

## Annual Salary Review

- Salaries are reviewed once a year, effective **April 1**.
- Increments are based on the employee's performance rating, position within the salary band, and market movement.
- Promotions approved during the annual review are also effective April 1. Off-cycle promotions require approval from the department head and the Chief People Officer.

## Salary Advances

- Employees may request a salary advance of up to **50%** of their monthly basic salary once in a 12-month period in case of emergencies.
- The advance is recovered in equal instalments over a maximum of **3 months**.

## Confidentiality of Pay

- Employees may discuss their own pay if they wish. Managers and People & Culture staff must treat individual pay information as strictly confidential.
