# Resignation and Offboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how to resign, the notice periods that apply, and the offboarding steps for employees leaving Lumora Systems.

## Notice Periods

| Employee group | Notice period |
|---|---|
| During probation | 2 weeks |
| Confirmed employees, job levels L1 to L4 | 1 month |
| Confirmed employees, job levels L5 to L6 | 2 months |
| Confirmed employees, job levels L7 and above | 3 months |

- Notice periods apply both when the employee resigns and when the company ends employment, except in cases of dismissal for gross misconduct.
- The company may, at its discretion, waive part of the notice period or pay the employee's basic salary in lieu of notice.

## How to Resign

1. Discuss the decision with the manager.
2. Submit a written resignation in LumoraHR under *Employment > Resign*, stating the proposed last working day.
3. People & Culture acknowledges the resignation in writing within **3 working days**, confirming the last working day.

## Using Leave During the Notice Period

- Annual leave may be used during the notice period only with the manager's approval.
- Sick leave taken during the notice period must be supported by a medical certificate.

## Knowledge Transfer

- The employee and manager agree a handover plan within **5 working days** of the resignation being accepted.
- Documentation, code, customer information and ongoing work must be transferred to the nominated colleagues before the last working day.

## Exit Interview

- People & Culture invites every leaving employee to a confidential exit interview, normally held in the final two weeks of employment.

## Return of Company Property

- On or before the last working day, employees must return the laptop, access card, corporate credit card and any other company property to IT & Security or Facilities.
- The cost of company property that is not returned may be recovered from the final settlement.

## Final Settlement

The final settlement is paid within **30 days** of the last working day and includes:

- Salary up to and including the last working day.
- Payment for unused annual leave for the current leave year (carried-forward leave that has not been used is also included if it has not lapsed).
- Gratuity, for employees with 5 or more years of service, as described in the Benefits Policy.
- Any approved and outstanding expense reimbursements.

Deductions may include outstanding salary advances, unrecovered allowances, and the cost of unreturned company property.

## Service Letter and EPF/ETF

- A service letter confirming employment dates and last held position is issued with the final settlement.
- Forms required to claim EPF and ETF balances are certified by People & Culture on request after the last working day.

## Rehire

- Former employees who left in good standing are eligible for rehire. Employees rehired within **12 months** of leaving may have their previous service recognised for leave entitlement purposes.
