# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

The Code of Conduct describes the standards of behaviour expected of everyone who works for or on behalf of Lumora Systems. It reflects our core values: Customer Obsession, Integrity First, Ownership, Learn and Share, and Inclusive by Default.

All employees must read and acknowledge the Code of Conduct in LumoraHR when they join and **every year** thereafter.

## Respect and Inclusion

- We treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect and dignity.
- Discrimination on the basis of race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, marital status, pregnancy, political opinion or any other protected characteristic is prohibited.
- Bullying, intimidation and abusive behaviour, whether in person, in writing or online, are not tolerated.

## Anti-Harassment

- Sexual harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. This includes unwelcome physical contact, comments, jokes, messages, images or advances of a sexual nature.
- Lumora Systems has an **Anti-Harassment Committee** chaired by a senior woman leader and including at least one external member. Complaints can be made to any committee member, to People & Culture, or through the ethics hotline.
- Complaints are investigated promptly and confidentially. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a serious disciplinary offence.

## Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of the company.
- Conflicts must be declared in LumoraHR under *Compliance > Declarations*. Examples include a financial interest in a supplier or competitor, a close relative working for a supplier, or managing a close relative.
- Secondary employment or freelance work requires written approval from the manager and People & Culture. Approval will not be given for work for a competitor or work that uses company time, equipment or confidential information.

## Gifts and Hospitality

- Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from business contacts with a value of up to **LKR 15,000** (or equivalent) per occasion.
- Gifts above this value must be declared in LumoraHR within **5 working days** and may need to be returned.
- Cash, cash equivalents (such as gift cards) and gifts during an active procurement process must never be accepted.

## Anti-Bribery and Corruption

- Offering, giving, requesting or accepting bribes or facilitation payments is strictly prohibited, in every country where we operate.
- All payments to third parties must be properly approved and accurately recorded.

## Confidential Information and Data Protection

- Employees must protect confidential information belonging to the company, customers and partners, both during and after employment.
- Personal data must be handled in line with the company's Data Protection Policy and applicable laws, including the Personal Data Protection Act of Sri Lanka and the UK GDPR where relevant.
- Suspected data breaches must be reported to the IT & Security team immediately.

## Use of Company Assets and AI Tools

- Company equipment, systems and accounts are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere with work or breach any policy.
- Only AI tools approved by IT & Security may be used with company or customer data. Customer data and source code must never be entered into unapproved AI tools.

## Social Media

- Employees must not disclose confidential information on social media or imply that personal views represent the company.
- Only authorised spokespersons may speak to the media on behalf of the company. Media enquiries should be forwarded to press@lumora-systems.example.

## Health and Safety

- Employees must follow workplace health and safety instructions and report accidents, near misses and hazards to Facilities & Administration.
- Working under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs is prohibited.

## Raising Concerns (Speak Up)

- Employees are encouraged to raise concerns about possible breaches of this Code with their manager, People & Culture, Legal & Compliance, or anonymously through the ethics hotline at ethics@lumora-systems.example.
- Lumora Systems does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Breaches of the Code

- Breaches of this Code may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, under the Grievance and Disciplinary Policy.
